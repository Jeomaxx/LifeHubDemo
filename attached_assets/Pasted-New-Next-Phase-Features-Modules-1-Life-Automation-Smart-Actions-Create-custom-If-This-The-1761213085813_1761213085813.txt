New Next-Phase Features & Modules
🌤️ 1. Life Automation & Smart Actions

Create custom “If-This-Then-That” (IFTTT-like) automation rules.

Example: “If salary is received → move 10% to savings + update goals.”

Connect actions across modules (Finance → Goals → Calendar).

Trigger automations via AI insights or manually.

Visual Automation Builder UI.

📈 2. Life Analytics & Insight Engine

Cross-module data analytics engine.

Generate weekly and monthly “Life Reports” with AI commentary.

Life Balance Score (Health + Finance + Productivity + Mood).

Correlation graphs: “Sleep vs Productivity,” “Spending vs Stress.”

Export full life analytics as PDF or Excel reports.

🤝 3. Collaboration & Shared Spaces

Invite family, friends, or coworkers to shared dashboards.

Assign shared tasks, bills, or goals.

Shared notes and collaborative plans.

Role-based permissions and secure data boundaries.

🏦 4. Smart Finance & Business Suite

Add “Small Business Manager” module.

Income & expense analytics for freelancers and startups.

AI invoice generator and tracker.

Tax calculator and document uploader.

Multi-currency wallet support.

🧭 5. Life Navigation & Planning

Long-term Life Roadmap module.

Create 1-year, 5-year, and 10-year vision plans.

AI progress predictor: “At current pace, you’ll reach Goal X in 8 months.”

Link long-term goals to existing modules (career, finances, health).

🧠 6. Knowledge Graph & Memory Center

AI-powered personal knowledge graph linking notes, documents, and activities.

Semantic search: “Show everything related to stress management.”

Visual mind-map display of relationships between topics.

📅 7. Event & Reminder System 2.0

Advanced reminder scheduling (recurring + smart triggers).

Smart snooze (“Remind me when I’m at home” / “Remind me when I have free time”).

Integrate with external calendars (Google, Outlook).

Voice command support.

🌐 8. External Integrations Layer

Add integration manager in Settings.

APIs for Google Fit, Notion, Drive, Outlook, Telegram.

Allow webhook triggers for automations and notifications.


10. AI Digital Twin

Build a “Digital Twin” model that learns user patterns.

Simulate daily decisions: “If you skip gym today, your stress score may increase by 8%.”

Use anonymized internal data for prediction and planning.

💡 11. Energy & Focus Manager

Track energy levels, focus times, and rest periods.

Suggest optimal task scheduling windows.

AI-based “Work Rhythm” coach: recommends breaks or focus sessions.

🛍️ 12. Asset & Subscription Manager

Track owned assets (devices, furniture, etc.).

Depreciation tracking and maintenance reminders.

Subscription cost optimization: “Cancel X, you’ll save $20/month.”

💬 13. Communication & Journaling Suite

Daily reflection journal with AI emotional summarization.

Voice journal recording and transcription.

Smart quote & memory generator — “On this day last year…”

🌱 14. Sustainability & Eco Tracker

Track environmental impact: power use, transport, purchases.

AI sustainability tips (“Switch to greener travel once a week”).

🧭 15. AI Scenario Simulator

“What if” simulations:

“What if I invest $500 monthly?”

“What if I sleep 1 hour more daily?”

Predict outcomes and display impact graphs.
