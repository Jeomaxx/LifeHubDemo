New and Expanded Advanced Intelligence Modules
💹 1. AI-Driven Financial Forecasting

Predict future income, expenses, and savings using historical data.
Show cash-flow trends, forecast scenarios (Optimistic / Realistic / Pessimistic), and generate budget adjustment suggestions.
Add a Forecast Dashboard in /modules/finances/forecast/ with Excel/PDF export.

😊 2. Mood Tracker & Emotional Insights

Daily mood logs (emoji + notes), sentiment analysis, mood trends graph, and correlation with tasks, bills, or sleep.
Suggest wellness actions and support optional encrypted storage in /modules/health/mood/.

🎯 3. Goal Achievement Engine

Create SMART goals linked across modules (finance → savings, health → gym, productivity → tasks).
Show predictive success rates, progress charts, motivational AI feedback, and milestone badges.
Module path /modules/productivity/goals/.

🔮 4. Life Event Predictor

Use cross-module data to anticipate upcoming life patterns (financial stress, productivity dips, burnout signals).
Display an “Upcoming Life Moments” timeline with confidence levels and preventive recommendations.
Module path /modules/assistant/life_events/.

💞 5. AI Relationship Insights

Analyze communication patterns, shared events, and emotional trends to enhance relationships.
Show “Connection Health Scores,” highlight important dates, and suggest actions (“Reach out to X”).
Integrate with Planner & Gifts at /modules/social/relationships/.

🌐 6. AI Life Advisor (NEW)

Goal: Deliver a unified, daily, personalized “life operating system.”

Features:

Aggregate insights from Finances, Health, Mood, Goals, Relationships.

Generate daily action plans, including:

“Pay your electricity bill today.”

“Drink 1.5 L more water to reach your goal.”

“Schedule a break — mood declining this week.”

Create a Daily Briefing card on Dashboard (summary of day’s priorities + recommendations).

Support voice playback (“Good Morning briefing”).

Allow manual feedback (mark suggestion helpful / not).

Module path /modules/assistant/life_advisor/.

Option for scheduled cron job to auto-generate daily plan emails or Telegram messages.

Each module → /modules/<module_name>/ with controller.php, model.php, view.php.
APIs in /routes/api.php; shared helpers in /core/helpers/data_bridge.php.
Database migrations for each.
Sidebar updates:

💰 Finances → Forecasting

❤️ Health → Mood Tracker
